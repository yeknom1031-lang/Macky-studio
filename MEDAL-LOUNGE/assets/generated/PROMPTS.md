# 生成アセット・プロンプト

組み込み image_gen を使用。生成結果をそのままゲーム内背景、ステンレス材質、メダル面へ使用。手描きの代用品ではありません。

## arcade_background

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: portrait background plate for a realistic Japanese arcade medal pusher simulator. Produce a photographic, softly out-of-focus Japanese game-center interior at adult eye height, looking slightly downward. Restrained navy, graphite and muted warm amber; fluorescent ceiling reflections and indistinct arcade cabinets only at the far left/right edges. The CENTER 80 percent must be very dark and empty low-contrast space behind the foreground game cabinet; no foreground machine, no medals or roulette, no people, no lettering, no logo, no watermark, no HUD. A real modest Japanese arcade, not a luxury casino palace, not sci-fi. Vertical 9:16 composition. Authentic ambient photographic lighting and deep but natural shadows.

## brushed_steel

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless tileable PBR base-color texture for the steel playfield and rails of a real Japanese coin pusher. A square edge-to-edge orthographic macro scan of satin brushed stainless steel, fine horizontal brushing, extremely subtle hairline scratches and finger-wear, neutral medium silver gray. Uniform diffuse cross-polarized illumination, no baked highlights or light gradient, no shadows, no bevel, no border, no object, no labels, no logo, no text, no watermark. Real used-but-well-maintained arcade machinery, not heavily corroded. Texture fills entire square with seamless edges and very fine detail.

## silver_medal

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: flat coin face texture for a realistic 3D arcade token. Exactly one silver-colored Japanese game-center medal, viewed perfectly straight on orthographically, absolutely circular and centered, diameter exactly 90 percent of square image. Neutral solid medium gray background. Actual nickel-plated steel with subtle circulation wear, fine machining hairlines, double raised concentric rims and a ring of tiny beaded dots. Embossed central classical laurel wreath surrounding a simple geometric monogram M, tasteful small engraved text 'MEDAL' along upper inner arc and 'AMUSEMENT' along lower arc. Mature, realistic stamped token not fantasy currency, no gold, no diamonds, no star icon, no denomination. Soft even diffuse lighting with shallow embossed relief visible, minimal directional shadows, no perspective, no thickness visible, no tilted coin, no extra objects, no watermark.
