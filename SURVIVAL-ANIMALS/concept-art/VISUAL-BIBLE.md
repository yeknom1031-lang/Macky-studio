# SURVIVAL ANIMALS — Visual Bible

## Core direction

- Premium stylized 3D action-survival presentation designed for a browser-first Three.js game.
- Tropical archipelago, turquoise ocean, monumental ancient ruins, volcanic islands, waterfalls, atmospheric fog, and strong day/night contrast.
- Painterly-realistic materials with readable silhouettes rather than strict photorealism.
- Third-person camera with a restrained, game-readable HUD.

## Main character

- Young Japanese explorer with short black hair.
- Tan utility jacket over a navy shirt, rust-orange scarf, dark cargo pants, leather gloves, and a round canvas backpack.
- Starts with a handmade spear and gradually unlocks tools, saddles, sea travel, and aerial travel.

## Signature companion

**Emberfox** is a charcoal-black fox-wolf with a bright orange flame-like mane, turquoise eyes, and glowing ember markings. It is the visual anchor for combat, creature care, traversal, and the final battle.

## UI language

- Translucent deep-navy glass panels.
- Turquoise for navigation, stamina, selection, and friendly systems.
- Orange for actions, danger, fire, and important confirmations.
- Rounded panels, clean pictograms, generous spacing, and minimal persistent text.

## Production target

The first playable build should reproduce the visual hierarchy and emotional tone of these images at a smaller scale: one dense island, Emberfox, one wild monster, a basic camp, a day/night cycle, and a complete weaken-and-capture loop.
