Issues found and corrected during this pass
- Camera shake accumulated during hitstop -> render-only offset restored.
- Enemy heavy damage occurred before the visible slam -> phase mapped to clip contact.
- Death timer froze after boarding -> update all dying enemies before mode/island early-outs.
- Dead pose survived reset -> animate neutral in syncWorld.
- Bow consumed counter but omitted power -> retain multiplier on projectile; ammo spent at release.
- Delayed boss victory leaked into new sessions -> simulation timer cleared by resetBattle.
- Boss HUD overlapped combat notice -> move notice lower for boss fights.
- Short effects aged before their first visible frame -> fresh slots and longer trails.
- Hit sparks emitted into victim geometry -> contact point moved to struck surface and spray toward attacker.

Limitations
This is an improved local stylized browser game, not equivalent to a commercial AAA production. Browser wall frame timing varied substantially during automation (roughly47-80ms snapshots); no60FPS claim or before/after performance claim. New animation adds no character meshes and effects are bounded. No new generated images; budget remains26 used /974 remaining.
